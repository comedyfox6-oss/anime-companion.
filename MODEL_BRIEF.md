# Character model brief

Use [references/blue-boy.jpg](./references/blue-boy.jpg) as a visual guide only: youthful masculine anime style, deep cobalt layered hair and long fringe, pale cool eyes, calm slightly tired expression, black tailored jacket, white shirt, vivid blue tie, dark blue-violet lighting.

Required asset: genuine humanoid **VRM 1.0** at public/models/boy.vrm. It must have mapped humanoid bones (head, neck, spine, arms, hands, legs, feet), blink and expression morphs, eye look-at, neutral standing pose, floor-level origin and forward-facing orientation. Keep topology and textures mobile-friendly.

Do not substitute a flat image, billboard, primitive mannequin or fake procedural geometry for the final model. Until a real VRM is supplied, explicitly show that the model is missing and label the image as a visual reference. The reference is not a 3D model and is not used to identify a real person.
